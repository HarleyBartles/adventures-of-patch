# Adventure readiness contract

## States

- `seed-ready`: the topic is useful but teaching/frame decisions remain open.
- `frame-ready`: the world, lesson, anti-pattern, positive pattern, Patch role,
  mappings, continuity, progression, and audience application are evidenced.
- `asset-ready`: the visual language and required reusable references are
  inspected against their intended use and packaged with provenance.
- `runbook-ready`: frame-ready and asset-ready; a requested downstream artifact
  workflow can begin without inventing foundations.

## Frame gate

A green frame is a teaching machine, not decorative theme. It establishes:

- a bounded world capable of producing concrete situations;
- the real operating lesson and recognisable failure/success patterns;
- Patch's character role without turning Patch into an execution owner;
- cast-to-function, environment-to-domain, and prop/state mappings;
- a continuity object or observable state transition;
- ordered progression and an audience action.

Generic advice, slogans, or a visually attractive theme are not frame evidence.

## Asset gate

Asset-ready requires only the references the frame actually needs. Each must
have an inspection basis, selection decision, provenance/package state,
and any required human approval. A source zip, contact sheet, asset sheet,
thumbnail, or generated-only candidate does not prove readiness by itself.

## Readiness evidence

Record source issue/brief, state, passed and failed gates, frame basis, asset
basis, inspection status, provisional/canonical status, blocker, and next owner.

## Boundary

Do not advance from a missing state, invent approval, or imply that readiness
commits the project to a particular downstream format.
