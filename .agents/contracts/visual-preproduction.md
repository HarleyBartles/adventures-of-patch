# Visual preproduction contract

## Entry and purpose

Visual preproduction starts from frame-ready evidence and makes the required
world, cast, environments, props, continuity objects, interactions, and visual
language repeatable for any downstream use.

## Source discovery

Start from `assets/INDEX.md`, follow the generated mesh, inspect the relevant
canon/style/bible/source surfaces, and record both used and skipped references.
When Patch appears, include current `assets/canon/patch/` references.

## Preflight packet

Name the artifact lane and intended use, composition, physical logic,
continuity, allowed text, positive constraints, hard negatives, reference
basis, known uncertainty, and inspection criteria. Keep operator context—issue
comments, candidate labels, paths, review prose, and process checklists—out of
audience-facing images.

## Bible-first asset families

New reusable asset classes begin with the smallest useful provisional bible.
Create an overview first, incorporate accepted learning, then expand member
assets and lock or explicitly retain provisional status. Do not collapse class
definition and family expansion into one omnibus artifact.

## Patch-first proof

For a new Patch-bearing world, first prove canonical Patch can inhabit and
interact with it while other characters/systems remain visually distinct.

## Candidate and review cadence

Generate or edit one governed candidate, stop for human inspection, then route
the latest candidate against the intended use and project canon. A clear repair decision may
produce one further candidate before the next stop. Repeated structural failure
requires a fresh generation from the current bible and accepted references.

## Asset-ready evidence

Record lane, inspected sources, bible/preflight state, candidate identifiers,
inspection and selection decisions, accepted references, package/provenance state, approvals,
remaining requirements, and readiness judgment.

## Boundaries

Generation is not acceptance. Selection is not canonisation. Reusable
references do not silently become another artifact type or output commitment.
