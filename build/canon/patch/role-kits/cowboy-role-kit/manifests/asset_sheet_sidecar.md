## Guidance
- Keep Patch recognisable: teal hoodie, hood antennae, off-white face panel, black oval eyes and black crossbody bag with > mark.
- Cowboy role reads through brown hat, leather waistcoat over the hoodie, boots, lasso and sheriff-star prop language.
- Do not replace the hoodie with western clothing; the waistcoat sits over it and preserves the Patch silhouette.
- Use side, back, action, close, detail and prop views as reference anchors for later scene generation.
- Preferred presentation is clean white-background source art with readable full-body and detail views.
