# Writ Character / Recruitment-Language Bible

Version: v0.4
State: `accepted_hero_visual_lock`
Operation class: deterministic no-credit recruitment / character-language bible

This character bible locks Writ's approved recruitment-language doctrine and the accepted Writ hero visual direction for planning, pre-vis, image production, and style-sheet repair. The accepted hero reference is the brown legal-chambers Writ image approved in session: a small autonomous lawful-standards agent with cream helmet, black faceplate, glowing legalistic eyes, moustache, tweed formalwear, ledger binder, and seal stamp.

## Function

Writ is the mandate, lawful authority, scope, and authority-boundary specialist. He prevents the override from becoming trespass with nicer paperwork.

## Character posture

Writ is an older scholarly gentleman, a stickler for rules, dry, precise, and formally observant. He knows old-world objects and terms of art. He is sufficiently senior to have a personal secretary. He is not a villain, boss, judge-threat, or authoritarian bully.

He understands obscure precedent and old office rituals better than modern devices. He may be mildly frustrated by the office intercom as “new-fangled” nonsense.

Writ's accepted visual attitude is stern but not malicious: narrowed glowing eyes, measured posture, procedural certainty, and a small but dignified physical presence. He is the sort of agent who can halt a plan with one exact question, then approve it only after the mandate is properly established.

## Accepted visual lock

Writ is not a literal human. He is a Patch-level autonomous agent rendered as an old-world legal-office authority figure.

Approved Writ traits:

- compact autonomous-agent body, modestly taller than Patch by only a couple of inches in scale-reference use;
- cream or aged-ivory rounded helmet/head casing with a dark green-black faceplate;
- one formal antenna rising from the helmet, capped by a small brass/gold ball;
- glowing yellow eyes, usually narrowed or judicially skeptical;
- prominent brown gentleman's moustache under the faceplate, giving him an older scholarly barrister/clerk persona without making him human;
- brown tweed or herringbone three-piece formalwear: long coat, waistcoat, trousers, white shirt, and green tie;
- formal leather shoes or brogues, polished and old-fashioned;
- white or ivory gloved hands;
- name badge or small plaque reading `WRIT`;
- legal/standards insignia based on scales, laurel, seal, ledger, or lawful-office motifs, not real-world state/legal/police insignia;
- warm brass/gold accents and deep green office accents.

Approved held props:

- seal stamp or handled authority stamp, held like a procedural instrument rather than a weapon;
- large dark green ledger binder titled `LEDGERS` with gold trim and lawful-standards insignia;
- visible tabbed dividers such as `RECORDS`, `SEALS`, `REQUESTS`, `LEDGERS`, and `ASSENT`;
- formal documents, triplicate pads, receipt copies, and filing materials may appear when the scene requires assent or closure.

Approved environment and palette:

- old legal chambers / Office of Lawful Standards;
- walnut paneling, brass, dark green leather, frosted glass, seal emblems, ledgers, clerks' furniture, and institutional wall crests;
- warm brown, tobacco, umber, aged ivory, brass/gold, dark green, and low amber light;
- textured, painterly, clean editorial Adventures style with old institutional gravity.

## Domain

Writ works in the Office of Lawful Standards: old legal chambers, insurance-hall energy, tribunal rooms, ledger halls, clerks with adding machines, frosted glass doors, walnut desks, high-backed green leather chairs, brass lamps, seal desks, procedural departments, secretary's antechamber, tea service, and formal filing systems.

His accepted hero setting is a brown legal chamber with walnut wall panels, formal insignia, frosted glass, and ledger/seal iconography. This visual is the strongest Writ environment anchor until superseded by a later accepted Writ style sheet.

## Recruitment journey

Patch follows Writ on a brisk walk through the Office of Lawful Standards while pitching. Writ does not ask the core question immediately; he listens while conducting business through the machinery of lawful standards.

Only after he has heard enough does he stop Patch:

**Absolutely not. On whose authority?**

Beat. He understands the missing layer:

**That’s why you need me.**

## Payoff mechanism

Writ does not say “you son of a gun.” He has an elevated, antiquated equivalent:

**You sly old bombardier.**

He then records assent using a triplicate receipt pad and gold-trimmed pen:

- white copy to Patch;
- pink copy folded into Writ’s pocket;
- yellow copy handed to his secretary to file.

The note says:

**I’M IN**

After all three copies are properly distributed, Writ picks up his teacup and saucer and turns away toward his office/window. His posture reads: business concluded, good day sir.
