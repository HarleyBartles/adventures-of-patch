## Asset overview

The Identity Emporium Shopkeeper is the human proprietor of the Identity Emporium.

He is a friendly, observant older shopkeeper who understands costumes, props, paperwork, and role selection. He is not a wizard, not a magical transformation guide, not a corporate trainer, and not a villainous trickster. He runs a literal costume-and-prop shop with warm commercial charm.

He should read as:

- human;
- older;
- kind but perceptive;
- slightly comic;
- professionally theatrical;
- grounded in retail craft;
- visually tied to the Identity Emporium palette.

## Function and narrative role

The shopkeeper represents the social and practical handoff at the Identity Emporium.

He helps visitors choose the visible surface of a role: costume pieces, props, tags, forms, bags, and mission-route paperwork. He can nudge, question, or scrutinise a role choice, but he must not appear to magically install competence.

Narrative function:

- greets Patch or other visitors;
- works behind the counter;
- explains available role kits;
- hands over paper bags, tags, forms, or route cards;
- inspects whether a chosen identity fits the mission;
- provides gently amused retail wisdom;
- keeps the Emporium warm, human, and socially grounded.

Key narrative rule:

The shopkeeper sells and curates role surfaces. He does not complete the mission, confer mastery, or become the source of the lesson.

## Visual style

Use the Adventures of Patch clean illustrated style, adapted for a human shopkeeper:

- friendly editorial cartoon construction;
- clear human silhouette;
- readable expression and gesture;
- warm, softly shaded illustration;
- thick clean outlines;
- simplified but characterful face;
- no photorealistic skin or uncanny realism;
- no grimdark fantasy merchant rendering;
- no glossy 3D mascot rendering.

He should fit naturally inside the Identity Emporium environment: green painted wood, warm aged timber, brass, cream paper, amber light, shop counter, tags, bags, route cards, and practical costume inventory.

## Core appearance

From the world proof, preserve these identity-critical features:

- older human shopkeeper;
- bald crown / high bare forehead;
- dark curly hair around the sides and back;
- thick black moustache;
- round wire glasses;
- prominent rounded nose;
- warm, curious eyes behind glasses;
- cream long-sleeved shirt;
- burgundy waistcoat / vest;
- dark green bow tie with small dot/detail pattern;
- yellow / brass measuring tape draped around the neck or over the shoulder;
- gentle, slightly amused expression;
- behind-counter working posture.

Allowed full-body extrapolation:

- dark brown or charcoal trousers;
- dark sensible shop shoes;
- optional sleeve garters or cuff detail;
- optional small watch chain, pencil, tags, or pin;
- optional apron only if it does not erase the burgundy waistcoat and measuring tape identity.

Do not change the core face/hair/moustache/glasses/waistcoat/bow-tie/measuring-tape read.

## Palette

Anchor the character to the Identity Emporium palette:

- Cream shirt: warm cream / off-white.
- Waistcoat: burgundy / warm brown-red.
- Bow tie: deep green with small warm dots or subtle pattern.
- Hair and moustache: dark brown / black.
- Glasses: brass or dark wire.
- Measuring tape: muted yellow / aged cream with dark markings.
- Accessories: brass, cream paper, dark wood, green shop accents.
- Trousers: dark brown, charcoal, or muted warm grey.

Avoid:

- bright modern retail colors;
- neon;
- royal wizard purple as a dominant read;
- black villain costume;
- superhero costume;
- corporate blue office palette;
- Patch teal as his dominant identity color.

## Pose and expression system

Useful source-image poses:

1. Behind the counter, leaning forward with hands together, warmly scrutinising a visitor's role choice.
2. Presenting a paper bag or parcel labelled as an identity kit, with one hand supporting the bag and the other gesturing.
3. Holding or reviewing a mission route card / clipboard, as if checking whether role, tools, and destination align.
4. Neutral full-body or three-quarter pose with measuring tape visible, calm shopkeeper stance.
5. Small expression set: welcoming, curious, thoughtful, amused, gently sceptical.

Expression range:

- welcoming;
- curious;
- amused;
- thoughtful;
- gently sceptical;
- professionally reassuring.

Avoid:

- sinister grin;
- magical trance;
- aggressive salesman;
- clownish buffoonery;
- stern judge;
- smug manipulator;
- blank generic NPC expression.

## Extractable Prompt and QA Blocks

### Prompt-positive canon block

Create the Identity Emporium Shopkeeper from Adventures of Patch as an older human costume-and-prop shop proprietor, grounded in the existing Patch-in-world Identity Emporium proof. He has a bald crown and high forehead, dark curly hair around the sides and back, a thick black moustache, round wire glasses, a prominent rounded nose, warm curious eyes, and a friendly slightly amused expression. He wears a cream long-sleeved shirt, burgundy waistcoat, dark green bow tie, and a yellow/cream measuring tape draped around his neck or shoulder. Render him in the clean friendly Adventures editorial illustration style with thick rounded outlines, soft shading, readable human expression, and warm Identity Emporium materials: green painted wood, aged timber, brass, cream paper, amber light, counter props, tags, bags, receipts, route cards, and costume-shop details.

### Prompt-negative canon block

Do not redesign the shopkeeper from scratch. Do not make him young, villainous, wizard-like, armoured, magical, corporate, sci-fi, photorealistic, glossy 3D, or generic fantasy merchant. Do not remove the bald crown, dark curly side hair, thick moustache, round glasses, cream shirt, burgundy waistcoat, green bow tie, or measuring tape. Do not give him Patch's teal hoodie, antennae, bag, face panel, `>` symbol, or Patch-like robot/body construction. Do not include Patch, customers, other agents, crowds, magical transformation effects, identity terminals, RPG shop weapons, swords, shields, armour, potions, spellbooks, or process/QA/repo text. Do not generate final sheet typography, caption bands, notes panels, or full asset-sheet layout in source images.

### Sensitive detail micro-specs

Face:
Older human face with high forehead / bald crown, dark curly side hair, thick black moustache, round wire glasses, rounded nose, warm eyes. Keep him friendly and observant, not sinister.

Wardrobe:
Cream shirt, burgundy waistcoat, dark green bow tie, visible measuring tape. These are identity-critical. Full-body extrapolation may use dark trousers and sensible dark shoes.

Measuring tape:
The measuring tape is his strongest personal prop. It should drape naturally around his neck or shoulder and should not become a scarf, snake, chain, sash, or magical ribbon.

Retail props:
Route cards, tags, string, paper bags, receipt books, clipboards, brass bell, banker lamp, and counter drawers are appropriate. They should support shopkeeper work, not become doctrine panels.

Patch distinction:
The shopkeeper is a human counterpart, not Patch and not an agent-bot. No teal hoodie, no antennae, no crossbody bag with `>` mark, no robot body.

### QA hard-gate checklist

A shopkeeper source image fails if:

1. It does not clearly depict the same shopkeeper identity from the world proof.
2. The bald crown / side hair / thick moustache / round glasses facial read is missing or heavily changed.
3. The burgundy waistcoat, green bow tie, cream shirt, and measuring tape are missing or replaced by a different character design.
4. The shopkeeper reads as a wizard, villain, fantasy merchant, armourer, sci-fi operator, corporate trainer, or generic NPC.
5. Patch, other agents, customers, or crowd characters contaminate a shopkeeper-only source image.
6. The image uses Patch identity markers on the shopkeeper.
7. The style drifts into photorealism, glossy 3D, painterly fantasy realism, horror, or generic stock illustration.
8. The source image tries to be a full final sheet with captions, notes, process labels, or ornamental layout text.
9. The shopkeeper is not compatible with the warm Identity Emporium environment palette and material language.
10. Text in the image is non-diegetic, process-like, repo-like, or required to understand the character.

### Feature-specific repair blocks

Identity repair:
Preserve the pose and useful shop context. Repair the character to match the established world-proof shopkeeper: older human, bald crown, dark curly side hair, thick black moustache, round wire glasses, cream shirt, burgundy waistcoat, green bow tie, measuring tape, warm curious expression.

Wardrobe repair:
Preserve the face and pose. Restore the cream shirt, burgundy waistcoat, dark green bow tie, and measuring tape. Remove wizard robes, armour, corporate suit, modern retail uniform, sci-fi operator clothing, or Patch-like teal hoodie.

Tone repair:
Preserve the shopkeeper's design. Adjust expression and body language to warm, observant, gently amused, and helpful. Remove sinister, aggressive, smug, villainous, magical, or buffoonish expression.

Source-image repair:
Preserve the useful character rendering. Remove final-sheet text, caption bands, notes boxes, labels, process panels, and decorative layout. Return to a single focused source image suitable for later deterministic compile.

Environment compatibility repair:
Preserve the character. Put him in or near warm Identity Emporium materials: green painted wood, aged timber, brass bell/lamp, cream paper tags, route card, paper bags, counter, receipt book. Remove sci-fi terminals, armoury walls, magic effects, or unrelated shop settings.

### Extrapolation rules

Safe extrapolation:

- full-body pose below the counter;
- small pin, watch chain, pencil, or tag details;
- counter scenes;
- paper-bag handoff;
- clipboard/route-card review;
- expression close-ups;
- seated or standing counter posture.

Unsafe extrapolation:

- changing age, core face, moustache, glasses, hair pattern, or measuring tape;
- replacing him with a new Victorian shopkeeper design unrelated to the proof;
- making him magical;
- making him a villain;
- making him Patch-like;
- turning source imagery into a full sheet in one generation.

### Prompt guidance for Pass A source-image generation

For first generation, create 2-3 focused source images, not a final sheet.

Recommended source images:

1. Behind-counter waist-up pose: shopkeeper leaning forward, hands near route card, warm curious expression.
2. Identity kit handoff: shopkeeper presenting a cream paper bag or tagged parcel, measuring tape visible.
3. Full-body or three-quarter neutral pose: clear wardrobe, measuring tape, burgundy waistcoat, green bow tie, friendly shopkeeper stance.

Keep backgrounds simple and in-world. Use the Identity Emporium counter/interior as support but do not let the environment dominate the character.
