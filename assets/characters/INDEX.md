# assets/characters index

Character asset families for Adventures of Patch.

## Files in this directory

No direct character files are expected at this level. Navigate into subdirectories.

## Subdirectories

- `bouncers/` - bouncer supporting-character family. See `bouncers/INDEX.md`.
- `stakeholders/` - reusable stakeholder character class for cross-deck counterparties. See `stakeholders/INDEX.md`.

## Notes for agents

Supporting characters are optional. Do not force a character family into a deck unless the selected issue or deck plan needs it.

Stakeholders are reusable human counterparties who define needs, priorities, constraints, proof, adoption, risk, and approval around Patch. They should be selected for deck roles when the story needs accountable human counterparties rather than random background people.
