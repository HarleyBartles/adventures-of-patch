# Index Frame Bible

Version: v0.3  
State: `minimal_provisional_frame_language`  
Operation class: deterministic no-credit frame bible

## Function

Index's frame grammar makes provenance visible. The slide behaves like old documents spread across a table and walked through as a world.

## Frame domain

Index's panels are archive artifacts:

- folded blueprints;
- dog-eared corners;
- faded paper;
- pinholes and moth holes;
- overlapping document panels;
- clipped panels at the slide edge suggesting unrelated archive sub-stories just off-page;
- reveal windows where one paper partly exposes the crucial clue beneath.

## Motion logic

Panel motion is evidence excavation:

1. buried route;
2. source evidence;
3. candidate path;
4. serious old file carrying the payoff.

Patch and Index may walk across the paper trail, but the core read should remain provenance and source evidence, not adventure-map traversal.

## Positive constraint block

```text
Use Index's archive-document frame grammar. Panels behave like old documents, folded blueprints, dog-eared records, faded paper, marginalia, pinholes, overlapping sheets, clipped off-page fragments, and reveal windows. The sequence should feel like evidence excavation: buried route, source evidence, candidate path, and final serious file. Obscure and reveal evidence meaningfully through paper overlap and document edges.
```

## Negative constraint block

```text
Do not use clean modern UI panels, magical library pages, cursed documents, horror decay, random paperwork storms, or abstract data screens as Index's main frame grammar. Do not make her panel style feel like Klause's decision grid, Receipt's receipt rolls, or Silk's broken-boundary route testing.
```

## QA gates

- Does the slide feel like a walk through provenance?
- Do document panels reveal and obscure evidence meaningfully?
- Is the final clue grounded in a source artifact rather than exposition?
- Is the paper tactile without becoming grimy or horror-like?
- Is the frame grammar clearly distinct from the other recruits?
