# Klause Frame Bible

Version: v0.3  
State: `minimal_provisional_frame_language`  
Operation class: deterministic no-credit frame bible

## Function

Klause's frame grammar makes decision discipline visible. It is internal/processual, not a physical journey through a location.

## Frame domain

Klause uses a clean nine-panel grid. The grid represents his internal decision process, not physical task cards on a desk. Patch arrives with a pitch binder and a spring in his step. Klause does not physically sort anything. He mentally rejects options while Patch speaks.

## Motion logic

The panels progress through Klause's posture:

- not looking up;
- pen continuing on his own work;
- pen pauses;
- slight look up;
- growing attention;
- leaning back in the office chair;
- pen held to mouth in thought;
- realization.

Panel “green-ness” is conceptual, not necessarily literal green: viability increases through clarity, focus, and compositional confidence as unworkable options are mentally marked off.

## Positive constraint block

```text
Use Klause's clean nine-panel internal decision grid. The grid represents mental elimination and route selection, not a physical room traversal or sorted card table. Patch brings one pitch binder while Klause remains at his immaculate desk. The panel sequence is carried by posture changes: not looking up, pen moving, pen pause, slight look, growing attention, leaning back, pen to mouth, realization, and final stamp.
```

## Negative constraint block

```text
Do not make Klause leave his desk, conduct a room traversal, physically sort task cards, or use an artifact spread like Index. Do not turn the decision grid into Writ's institutional departments or Receipt's receipt rolls. Do not rely on literal green panels if viability can be shown by composition, attention, and clarity.
```
