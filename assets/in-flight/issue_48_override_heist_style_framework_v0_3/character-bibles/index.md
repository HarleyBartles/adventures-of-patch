# Index Character / Recruitment-Language Bible

State: `accepted_hero_first_pass_visual_anchor_plus_provisional_recruitment_language`  
Operation class: deterministic no-credit recruitment / character-language bible

This character bible locks approved recruitment-language doctrine for planning and pre-vis, and now records the accepted Index hero first-pass visual anchor. It does not yet lock a full turnaround, final style sheet, final anti-pattern sheet, or complete accepted asset-sheet canon.

## Function

Index is the design-history, source-evidence, provenance, and candidate-route specialist. She finds whether a lawful route exists in the original design trail rather than inventing a route from convenience.

## Character posture

Index is stoic, quiet, bookish, and mission-focused. She gives little away. She does not argue from confidence; she moves toward provenance. Her silence should create ambiguity: she might be helping, or she might be finding the file that proves the heist cannot be done.

Index should read as careful, observant, and exact, not warm exposition, theatrical certainty, or helpful-generic assistant energy. Her strongest visual action is source-evidence handling: lifting a document, revealing a buried route, checking marginalia, or quietly following the paper trail.

## Domain

Index's domain is old files, dusty wrinkled documents, design and revision plans, folded blueprints, dog-eared records, old notes, marginalia, and paper-trail evidence. It should feel archived, handled, and slightly neglected, not cursed, mouldy, or horror-like.

The archive supports her but must not consume her. For hero and character-reference imagery, keep Index character-first with tactile source documents as evidence. Use old blueprints, folded plans, dog-eared files, marginalia, red index tabs, and archive tags. Avoid magical books, cursed papers, data screens, random paperwork storms, modern dashboards, derelict horror, or generic library stacks.

## Accepted hero first-pass visual anchor

The accepted Index hero direction is a Patch-level autonomous-agent source-evidence specialist standing at an archive table, handling a folded blueprint or old source document as she reveals or checks a buried route.

Locked visual traits:

- Patch-level autonomous agent, compact and non-human;
- large smooth pale agent face/faceplate, not a human face;
- messy dark blue / muted blue top bun;
- two pencils twisted through and holding the bun in place;
- round implied-glasses eye language integrated into the face design;
- purplish-pink eye marks that move within the circular surrounds to indicate attention;
- small simple agent mouth;
- expression reads stoic, observant, bookish, and source-focused, not blank, cute-human, worried, or animal-like;
- long slouchy hooded cardigan;
- cardigan hood is down, visibly pooled/gathered around the back of her neck and shoulders;
- hood must not cover or obscure the bun or pencils;
- cardigan reads dowdy inherited archive clothing, not elderly, fashionable, sporty, tactical, or robe-like;
- cream/off-white shirt under cardigan;
- dark trousers;
- understated dark archive shoes/low boots, not teal trainers;
- no bag, crossbody strap, Patch pouch, or field satchel;
- warm paper/archive palette: paper cream, archive brown, muted blue, charcoal, small red tab accents;
- clean Adventures editorial/vector-adjacent character art, not photorealism, doll realism, glossy toy, or furry/animal style.

### Face and eye construction lock

Index's eyes should imply round glasses without literal glasses. The circular surrounds are part of the face/faceplate design, not removable eyewear.

The eye marks are simple flat autonomous-agent graphics, not human or animal eyes. They should avoid iris/pupil realism, catchlights, lashes, eyelid modelling, and glossy wet-eye construction. Character comes from graphic placement, attention direction, and small brow/face marks rather than human-eye rendering.

The accepted direction uses purplish-pink eye marks inside round adjacent circular surrounds. The eye marks may sit lower or to one side inside the circles to show she is looking down at evidence or following a route. Add only minimal graphic brow/attention marks when needed. Keep her observant and stoic, not blank, cute, worried, or sentimental.

### Hooded-cardigan construction lock

Index wears a long slouchy hooded cardigan. The hood is attached to the cardigan and worn down. It should be visible in hero art, not deferred to turnaround or implied by text.

The hood should read from the front as soft gathered cardigan fabric around the neck/shoulder line and from side/rear as a down hood mass at the upper back. The hood sits below the bun and pencils. It must not become a sporty hoodie, zip hoodie, tactical hood, cloak, robe hood, scarf, shawl, or cowl replacement.

The garment read is dowdy, inherited, practical archive-worker clothing. Index herself is not old. Her clothes may seem like they belonged to an older agent, but she should not become an elderly librarian caricature.

## Visual separation from other crew members

Index must not inherit another character's identity language.

- Not Patch: no teal hoodie, no two hood antennae, no crossbody bag, no `>` identity mark, no teal trainers, no cheerful protagonist face read.
- Not Silk: no stealth/crawlspace suit, no charcoal tactical hood, no threadline inspector gear, no ninja/assassin mood.
- Not Writ: no moustache, no tweed legal formality, no ledger/seal authority props, no judge/lawyer/courtroom read.
- Not Rollback: no bulky containment-worker mass, no olive utility suit, no Plan B/restore/failsafe body language.
- Not Klause: no immaculate decision desk, no office-navy zip hoodie, no white-shirt/tie decision-specialist posture, no stamp-as-decision role.
- Not Receipt: no audit-roll, receipt printer, proof-of-work checkout, or final accounting role.
- Not Bit/Bot: no simple helper-robot body, no generic robot mascot ontology.

Index's role palette is paper cream, archive brown, dowdy brown/grey knit, muted blue hair/hood element, pale agent face/hands, purplish-pink eye marks, notebook red tabs, and restrained charcoal soles/trousers.

## Recruitment journey

Patch arrives and asks his ask. Index says little or nothing. She turns and begins moving through the archive. Patch follows, still clarifying the plan as they travel across the paper trail. The journey should feel like a mission through paperwork, not a conversation at a desk.

The tension is that Index may be walking Patch toward the proof that blocks him. Instead she reaches the file she was seeking. The evidence speaks.

## Payoff mechanism

The final file or document, serious and old, contains the payoff line:

**You son of a gun, I’m in.**

The humour comes from the contrast between stoic archive archaeology and the direct heist-movie line appearing inside the document itself.
