# Silk Character / Recruitment-Language Bible

Version: v0.5 character-design rationalisation  
State: `minimal_provisional_character_language`  
Operation class: deterministic no-credit recruitment / character-language bible

This character bible locks approved recruitment-language doctrine and the current working visual direction for planning and pre-vis. It does not lock final character turnaround, final palette, or accepted asset-sheet canon.

Detailed Silk panel/frame grammar now lives in `../frame-bibles/silk_frame_bible.md`.

## Function

Silk is the red-team / adversarial pressure-testing specialist. She tests whether Patch's proposed lawful route leaks, contains unsafe shortcuts, or collapses under hostile interpretation.

## Character posture

Silk is precise, tense, quiet, observant, exacting, and adversarial without being villainous. She is spider-adjacent in motion, rigging, and motif, but must not become a literal spider, monster, or horror creature.

She gives little away. She is not impressed by confidence. She respects routes that survive pressure.

## Current working visual direction

Silk's current preferred design base is **Threadline Inspector A** from the character exploration pass:

- Patch-scale autonomous-agent body, not human-sized;
- grey hoodie / soft protective shell as the signature outer silhouette;
- compact, slightly crouched, careful working posture;
- pale simple faceplate with calm narrow dark eyes;
- **one antenna only** so she remains distinct from Patch's two-antenna identity;
- compact red-thread spool or reel pack;
- small hook, seam probe, clamp, line tag, chalk, and pressure-test tools;
- functional harness kept light and not over-busy;
- red used mainly for thread, rigging, clips, anchors, and small tool accents rather than costume pattern.

Borrow lightly from Clipwright for clamp/aperture tool language and from Quiet Pin for restraint and iconic simplicity. Do not average the whole set of candidates into a busy hybrid. Silk is primarily the grey-hoodie Threadline Inspector with a single antenna.

## Domain

Silk's domain is a red-team test corridor: narrow passages, tensioned red string, rappel drops, dead ends, switchbacks, crawl spaces, pressure plates, mock alarms, decoy readers, trap-like insets, blocked shortcuts, and final check pins.

For the current preferred frame-architecture direction, this test corridor should feel like an ordinary apartment corridor weaponised into a red-team test space: bare floorboards, plain plaster walls, cheap apartment doors, exposed conduit, taped wiring, bare hanging lightbulbs, utility cupboards, radiators, simple alarm boxes, maintenance hatches, improvised anchors, and small pressure-test devices.

Do not drift into cave, dungeon, bunker, sci-fi base, warehouse, cyberpunk maze, or horror labyrinth aesthetics.

## Relationship to Patch

Patch is open, approachable, protagonist-forward, friendly, and expressive. Silk is quieter, more inward, more technical, more skeptical, and more seam-aware.

Silk must not copy Patch's identity system. Do not give her Patch's teal hoodie, two antennae, crossbody bag, `>` symbol, full Patch silhouette, or protagonist colour read.

## Recruitment journey

Patch arrives and asks his ask. Silk gives little away, clips onto a line, and moves into the test corridor. Patch follows more casually, still pitching and clarifying. The route is tested in motion. Patch's proposal only earns attention when it survives the corridor.

Use `../frame-bibles/silk_frame_bible.md` for how that route and its panel boundaries behave.

## Payoff mechanism

The route reaches the final check-mark pin. The pin tag already says:

**I’m in**

Silk is surprised. A caption, not a thought bubble or speech bubble, says:

**You son of a gun.**

She hands Patch the **I’m in** tag.

## Positive constraint block

```text
Silk is a Patch-scale autonomous-agent red-team pressure tester. Her current working design is the grey-hoodie Threadline Inspector: compact crouched agent body, soft grey hood/shell, pale simple faceplate, calm narrow eyes, one antenna only, red test-thread spool, small hook/probe, light clamp kit, chalk or line tags, and quiet exacting posture. She is precise, tense, observant, and adversarial without being villainous. Red belongs mainly to thread, rigging, anchors, clips, and tool accents, not costume pattern. Patch asks his ask and follows while Silk pressure-tests the route; Silk joins only when the route survives.
```

## Negative constraint block

```text

Do not add extra arms, mandibles, spider abdomen, spider legs, fangs, horror webbing, or predatory menace. Do not give her a black-and-red superhero bodysuit, giant white vigilante eyes, web-pattern costume, ninja/assassin cues, katanas, Patch's teal identity, Patch's two antennae, Patch's crossbody bag, Patch's `>` symbol, or Patch's full silhouette. Do not overburden her with bulky gear; she should remain quiet, exact, compact, and functional.
```

## QA gates

- Does Silk read as a Patch-scale autonomous agent rather than a human, spider, monster, or superhero?
- Is the grey hoodie / soft shell the main silhouette?
- Does she have exactly one antenna, not Patch's two?
- Is red mostly thread/tool/rigging language rather than a costume pattern?
- Is the red-team pressure-testing role visible through posture and tools?
- Is she distinct from Patch, Bit, Bot, and generic helper robots?
- Does she feel precise, quiet, observant, and adversarial without villainy?
- Are spider-adjacent cues limited to rigging, tension, posture, and careful movement?
- Does the character bible stay focused on identity while frame grammar is delegated to `silk_frame_bible.md`?
