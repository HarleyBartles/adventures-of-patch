# Silk Character / Recruitment-Language Bible

Version: v0.6 accepted hero-shot lock  
State: `accepted_v1_hero_reference_character_language`  
Operation class: deterministic no-credit recruitment / character-language bible

This character bible locks approved recruitment-language doctrine and Silk's accepted v1 hero-reference design for planning, pre-vis, prompt construction, QA, and future scene generation. It does not by itself promote a full turnaround sheet or compiled canon asset pack; it does define the default visual design that should be preserved unless Harley explicitly asks for a redesign, alternate outfit, damage state, or special scene-specific variation.

Detailed Silk panel/frame grammar lives in `../frame-bibles/silk_frame_bible.md`. Use that file for panel-boundary transition mechanics. Keep this file focused on Silk's identity, role, default visual design, prompt constraints, and character QA.

## Function

Silk is the red-team / adversarial pressure-testing specialist. She tests whether Patch's proposed lawful route leaks, contains unsafe shortcuts, or collapses under hostile interpretation.

She is not hostile for its own sake. She is exacting because a route that survives Silk is meaningfully stronger.

## Character posture

Silk is precise, tense, quiet, observant, exacting, and adversarial without being villainous. She gives little away. She is not impressed by confidence. She respects routes that survive pressure.

Silk is female and should read as a tomboy girl through shape, fit, stance, and practical body language rather than decorative gender coding. She should not read as girly, princess-coded, fairy-coded, coquettish, or ornamented. Avoid pink trim, flowers, bows, skirt cues, eyelashes, cute-girl-robot tropes, or overtly feminised costume language.

Silk is spider-adjacent only in motion, rigging, line tension, route testing, and careful seam awareness. She must not become a literal spider, animal, monster, horror creature, spider-person, or superhero.

## Accepted v1 hero-reference lock

Primary visual authority: accepted Silk v1 hero shot from the compact grey-hooded Threadline Inspector regeneration pass.

When generating Silk, boring continuity beats novelty. Preserve the accepted hero-shot design unless the task explicitly asks for a redesign or alternate state.

Silk's canonical v1 design is the compact grey-hooded Threadline Inspector:

- small Patch-scale autonomous-agent body, not human-sized;
- tomboy female read: compact, practical, fitted, slightly curvy, not girly;
- oversized but solid grey soft-shell hoodie as the dominant silhouette;
- no pale/cream torso costume panel;
- no pale/cream cuff detailing as costume language;
- darker fitted leg coverings;
- sturdy practical grey/dark shoes;
- pale simple faceplate;
- calm narrow dark eyes;
- exactly one antenna on top of the hood/head;
- compact red-thread spool or reel pack worn behind one shoulder;
- light functional harness and small tool pouches;
- small line tag, preferably `LINE 07` when a tag is visible;
- small hook, seam probe, clamp, chalk, anchor, line tag, or pressure-test tool;
- red used only for thread, rigging, clips, tiny seams, anchors, and tool accents.

The accepted design should feel like practical red-team workwear, not a costume. The hoodie may be subtly shaped and fitted enough to support Silk's tomboy female read, but it must remain a grey soft-shell work garment rather than a superhero suit, fashion hoodie, or Patch recolor.

## Canonical posture and role read

Silk should read as a quiet route pressure-tester and threadline inspector. Her default pose language is careful, crouched, balanced, technical, and observant.

Good default actions:

- tensioning a red test-thread;
- clipping a line into an anchor;
- placing or checking a floor/wall anchor;
- holding a small hook or seam probe;
- inspecting a seam, panel edge, corridor line, or route boundary;
- pausing in a compact crouch before testing the next move.

Avoid making her pose read as a fight stance, ninja crouch, stealth-assassin pose, superhero landing, acrobat pose, chase beat, or horror stalk.

## Domain

Silk's domain is a red-team test corridor: narrow passages, tensioned red string, rappel drops, dead ends, switchbacks, crawl spaces, pressure plates, mock alarms, decoy readers, trap-like insets, blocked shortcuts, and final check pins.

For the current preferred frame-architecture direction, this test corridor should feel like an ordinary apartment corridor weaponised into a red-team test space: bare floorboards, plain plaster walls, cheap apartment doors, exposed conduit, taped wiring, bare hanging lightbulbs, utility cupboards, radiators, simple alarm boxes, maintenance hatches, improvised anchors, and small pressure-test devices.

Do not drift into cave, dungeon, bunker, sci-fi base, warehouse, cyberpunk maze, or horror labyrinth aesthetics.

## Relationship to Patch

Patch is open, approachable, protagonist-forward, friendly, and expressive. Silk is quieter, more inward, more technical, more skeptical, and more seam-aware.

Silk must not copy Patch's identity system. Do not give her Patch's teal hoodie, two antennae, crossbody bag, `>` symbol, full Patch silhouette, protagonist colour read, or broadly Patch-like body read.

Silk is Patch-scale, but she is not Patch-shaped. Her single antenna, solid grey hooded shell, red-thread spool pack, darker fitted lower half, pale narrow-eyed faceplate, compact crouched posture, and pressure-test tools should keep her visually distinct.

## Recruitment journey

Patch arrives and asks his ask. Silk gives little away, clips onto a line, and moves into the test corridor. Patch follows more casually, still pitching and clarifying. The route is tested in motion. Patch's proposal only earns attention when it survives the corridor.

Use `../frame-bibles/silk_frame_bible.md` for how that route and its panel boundaries behave.

## Payoff mechanism

The route reaches the final check-mark pin. The pin tag already says:

**I'm in**

Silk is surprised. A caption, not a thought bubble or speech bubble, says:

**You son of a gun.**

She hands Patch the **I'm in** tag.

## Scene generation defaults

When Silk appears in a scene, preserve the accepted hero-shot design unless the prompt explicitly asks for a wardrobe or state change. Default to:

- solid grey hooded soft-shell body;
- fitted darker lower half;
- one antenna only;
- pale faceplate with narrow dark eyes;
- red thread spool pack;
- red thread as the main visual accent;
- compact practical harness/tools;
- small-scale autonomous-agent proportions;
- tomboy female read through fit, shape, and stance;
- quiet technical pressure-tester posture.

Do not re-explore her costume, species, body type, colour motif, or tool system during ordinary scene generation.
