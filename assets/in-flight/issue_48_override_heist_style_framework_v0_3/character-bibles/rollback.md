# Rollback Character / Recruitment-Language Bible

State: `minimal_provisional_character_language`  
Operation class: deterministic no-credit recruitment / character-language bible

This character bible locks approved recruitment-language doctrine for planning and pre-vis. It does not lock final character appearance, full turnaround design, final palette, or accepted asset-sheet canon.

## Function

Rollback is the recovery, contingency, containment, abort-condition, restore-path, and Plan B specialist. He proves that a plan without recovery is not a plan.

## Character posture

Rollback is broad, physically imposing, Black, calm under pressure, practical, blunt, wry, and veteran-coded without being gun-happy or gung-ho. He is not a weapons character. His confidence comes from controlled preparedness.

## Domain

Rollback's domain is an active contingency bunker. It is not a static storage room. It has an observation deck overlooking running containment experiments and disaster simulations. The bunker is subtly too prepared.

Rooms begin with normal labels such as **Plan A** and **Plan B**, then jump to things like **Plan M**, then lose tidy alphabetic designation and become increasingly elaborate failure-on-failure scenarios.

## Recruitment journey

Patch follows Rollback along the observation deck while pitching. They pass active containment simulations. **Plan A** is catastrophically failing and must be locked down. Rollback barely reacts; he locks it down and a steel shutter snaps over the observation window.

Other rooms are stable and controlled. The sequence demonstrates that Rollback assumes Plan A failure is normal, while Patch has not brought even Plan B.

Rollback stops Patch:

**What’s your Plan B?**

Patch has none.

Rollback:

**Then you ain’t got no plan.**

The implied “fool” is felt, not spoken.

## Realization and payoff

Rollback's realization is not that Patch understands contingency. It is:

**That’s why you need me.**

He concedes with wry inevitability:

**You son of a gun.**

He slams a bunker control button. Every monitor and observation window flips to:

**I’M IN**

Rollback immediately walks on toward the next corridor of simulations, leaving Patch standing alone in a room glowing with synchronized **I’M IN** screens.

## Positive constraint block

```text
Rollback is a Patch-level autonomous-agent contingency and recovery specialist. He is broad, physically imposing, Black, calm under pressure, practical, blunt, wry, and veteran-coded without being gun-happy. His domain is an active contingency bunker with recovery simulations, Plan B / Plan C labels, controlled preparedness, and failure-on-failure rooms. He asks “What’s your Plan B?” and, when Patch has none, says “Then you ain’t got no plan.” He realizes this is exactly why Patch needs him, says “You son of a gun,” and joins.
```

## Negative constraint block

```text
Do not make Rollback a literal human soldier, gun-happy commando, weapons dealer, villain, bodyguard stereotype, animal, Patch clone, Bit/Bot-style robot, or generic strongman. Do not show weapons racks, tactical violence, war scenes, flags, real military insignia, or aggressive combat posing. Do not make the bunker a static storeroom instead of an active contingency environment.
```

## QA gates

- Does Rollback read as recovery/contingency, not weapons/military fantasy?
- Is he broad and calm rather than gung-ho?
- Does he expose Patch's lack of Plan B?
- Does his realization read as “that’s why you need me”?
- Is he clearly not Patch-like?
