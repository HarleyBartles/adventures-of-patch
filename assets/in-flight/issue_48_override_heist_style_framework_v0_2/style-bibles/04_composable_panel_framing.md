## Purpose

This bible defines shared panel-framing doctrine for the Override Heist. It sits above the individual character bibles and below the global world bible. Its core principle: **panel style is not decoration; panel style is a character's mode of reasoning made visible.**

## General panel rule

Panels should be readable at slide scale and should support cause-and-effect. Use comic-book grammar to expose reasoning and process, not to add noise.

## No-floating-arrow doctrine

Avoid floating arrows laid over panels. If attention or sequence needs direction, use panel geometry, route-line continuity inside the scene, lighting, object motion, character movement, or a shaped gutter/panel edge. If a panel sequence needs a pointer, shape the panel itself as the pointer.

## Panels as physical domain

For most recruits, panel layout is not merely a page design. It is the place Patch and the recruit physically travel:

- Index's panels are old documents and the paper trail Patch walks through.
- Silk's panels are the test corridor route Patch and Silk physically traverse.
- Rollback's panels are observation-window rooms along a containment inspection deck.
- Writ's panels are institutional lawful-authority departments and chambers.

Klause and Receipt are exceptions: their panel language is processual/internal rather than spatial.

## Default deck grammar

Use a clear classic comic rhythm as the default deck-safe panel language. Candidate 1 and Candidate 2 panel mockups provide the safer baseline: restrained rectangular panels, clean sequence, good cause-and-effect, controlled variation.

Use the more energetic Candidate 3 / Candidate 4 language selectively: action-shaped panels, angled heist montage, route-shaped panels, scan-beam panels, circular status insets, and burst panels should be reserved for strong process beats or signature slides.

## Character-specific panel grammars

- **Index:** archive document panels; dog-eared, folded, dusty, overlapping, clipped, with reveal-window occlusion.
- **Silk:** thin, tense route-testing panels; red string, dead ends, switchbacks, narrow crawl spaces, rappel drops.
- **Rollback:** chunky observation-deck panels; containment rooms, glass windows, shutters, big load-bearing borders.
- **Writ:** formal institutional panels; orderly rectangles, brass margins, office/chamber thresholds, triplicate-document logic.
- **Klause:** clean nine-panel internal decision grid; posture and panel viability show mental rejection.
- **Receipt:** three fat receipt-roll columns; minimal anti-recruitment, audit slip as hero.

## Planning-table reuse rule

The planning-table slide should reuse the six recruitment grammars as small inserts around the keycard. Each insert should read as a layer contributed by a recruit: paper-trail/provenance, stress-tested route, contingency recovery, authority mandate, selected decision, audit record.

## Prompt-positive canon block

```text
Use composable Override Heist panel framing where panel style expresses reasoning. Keep panels readable at slide scale with clean Adventures editorial-vector finish. Use panel geometry, gutters, route continuity, lighting, and object motion to direct attention rather than floating arrows. Let each recruit's panel language reflect their domain: archive documents for Index, tensile test route for Silk, containment observation deck for Rollback, orderly lawful chambers for Writ, nine-panel internal decision grid for Klause, and receipt-roll audit strips for Receipt. Use dynamic panel shapes only where the shape itself tells the process.
```

## Prompt-negative canon block

```text
Do not use chaotic unreadable comic pages, tiny overloaded panels, floating arrows over panels, dense dialogue, manga clutter, random sound effects, decorative panel shapes with no story function, or panel drama that hides the lesson. Do not make every recruit use the same panel rhythm. Do not use dynamic action panels as the default when calm editorial panels would explain the beat better.
```

## QA hard gates

- Does the panel style communicate process rather than decoration?
- Are panels readable at slide scale?
- Is the recruit's reasoning mode visible in the panel grammar?
- Are arrows avoided or integrated into panel shape/scene route?
- Is the sequence understandable without too much text?
- Does panel energy support the lawful-override lesson rather than overwhelm it?
