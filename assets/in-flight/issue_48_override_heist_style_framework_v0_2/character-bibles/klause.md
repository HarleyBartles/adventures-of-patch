# Klause Character / Recruitment-Language Bible


State: `minimal_provisional_character_language`  
Operation class: deterministic no-credit recruitment / character-language bible  

This character bible locks approved recruitment-language doctrine for planning and pre-vis. It does not lock final character appearance, full turnaround design, final palette, or accepted asset-sheet canon.


## Function

Klause is the decision specialist. He selects the workable route after evidence, pressure testing, authority, and contingency constraints make the option space smaller.

## Character posture

Klause is German, serious, studious, disciplined, and intolerant of wasted time. He does not leave his immaculate desk. He barely moves. His domain is decision discipline, not walking someone through a location.

## Domain

Klause's domain is an immaculate decision office: severe negative space, exact desk geometry, appointment logic, one aligned pen, controlled light, rigid order, and an atmosphere where preparation is evidence of decision-readiness.

## Panel grammar

Klause uses a clean nine-panel grid. The grid represents his internal decision process, not physical task cards on a desk. Patch arrives with a pitch binder and a spring in his step. Klause does not physically sort anything. He mentally rejects options while Patch speaks.

The panels progress through Klause's posture:

- not looking up;
- pen continuing on his own work;
- pen pauses;
- slight look up;
- growing attention;
- leaning back in the office chair;
- pen held to mouth in thought;
- realization.

Panel “green-ness” is conceptual, not necessarily literal green: viability increases through clarity, focus, and compositional confidence as unworkable options are mentally marked off.

## Recruitment journey

Patch arrives with a pitch binder and begins to pitch.

Klause, initially without looking up:

**Do you have an appointment?**

Then:

**If you don’t have an appointment, I doubt you have a decision. Come back when you're more prepared.**

Patch keeps pitching. Klause listens despite himself.

He later diagnoses:

**This plan has too many options. I’ve been rejecting them while you talk.**

The only workable route is not one Patch has already chosen. It is Klause's decision function.

## Realization and payoff

Klause's “son of a gun” moment reads as:

**Well played, sir. You win at my game.**

Spoken line:

**You son of a gun.**

He gestures for Patch's pitch binder. Patch hands it over. Klause does not open it. He stamps the front cover:

**I’M IN**

## Positive constraint block

```text
Klause is a Patch-level autonomous-agent decision specialist. His recruitment slide uses a clean nine-panel grid representing his internal decision elimination. Patch arrives energetic with a single pitch binder. Klause stays at his immaculate desk and barely moves. Early panels show him not looking up and mentally rejecting the pitch. Mid panels show his pen pause and his attention shift. Later panels show him leaning back, pen held to his mouth, evaluating. He diagnoses that the plan has too many options and that he has been rejecting them while Patch talks. He realizes the only workable option is where he comes in. With restrained “well played” energy he says “You son of a gun,” gestures for the pitch binder, does not open it, and stamps the cover “I’M IN.”
```

## Negative constraint block

Do not make him a literal human man, military officer, villain, authoritarian boss, angry caricature, animal, Patch clone, Bit/Bot-style robot, or generic analyst.
