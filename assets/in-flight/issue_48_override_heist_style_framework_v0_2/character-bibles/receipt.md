# Receipt Character / Recruitment-Language Bible


State: `minimal_provisional_character_language`  
Operation class: deterministic no-credit recruitment / character-language bible  

This character bible locks approved recruitment-language doctrine for planning and pre-vis. It does not lock final character appearance, full turnaround design, final palette, or accepted asset-sheet canon.


## Function

Receipt is the audit-trail, record, timestamp, reviewability, and proof-after-the-fact specialist. He ensures the override is not an invisible backdoor.

## Character posture

Receipt is neat, mild, precise, nerdy, quiet, and easy to underestimate. He is the anti-spectacle recruit. His power is that he has already been observing and recording everything before anyone noticed.

## Domain

Receipt's domain is a tiny audit alcove: receipt printer, paper rolls, timestamp clock, labelled trays, carbon copy, ledger-slate, pencil cup, stamp pad, small archive boxes, warm desk lamp, tidy drawers, and tiny filing rituals.

## Panel grammar

Receipt uses three fat receipt-paper rolls, not many thin panels. This avoids artificial filler and gives him a precise, economical visual grammar:

- Roll 1: Patch arrives ready to pitch.
- Roll 2: Receipt gives a mild “no need” head shake and hands over the slip.
- Roll 3: hero view of the actual receipt he handed Patch.

Each roll may contain two small printed-image panels, but the final roll should make the audit slip itself the hero.

## Recruitment structure

Receipt is the anti-recruitment. Patch arrives ready to pitch, but Receipt already knows. He has quietly recorded every prior recruitment angle: provenance, adversarial test, contingency, authority, and decision.

He simply shakes his head, tears off the receipt, and hands it to Patch.

## Payoff mechanism

The receipt records the observed canonical assent format:

- realization;
- “you son of a gun”;
- “I’m in.”

Writ's “you sly old bombardier” is logged as a lawful equivalent. Receipt reproduces the canonical format correctly because he has observed and recorded the pattern.

The receipt includes:

**YOU SON OF A GUN**  
**I’M IN**

with audit notation that the observed format is complete.

## Positive constraint block

```text
Receipt is a Patch-level autonomous-agent audit-trail specialist. His recruitment slide is an anti-recruitment in three fat receipt-paper rolls. Patch arrives ready to pitch; Receipt quietly shakes his head because no pitch is needed; he has already observed and recorded the prior recruitment angles. Receipt tears off and hands Patch the audit slip. The final roll is a hero view of the receipt, which records the observed canonical assent format: “YOU SON OF A GUN” and “I’M IN,” with Writ’s “you sly old bombardier” treated as a logged lawful equivalent. The slide should feel tiny, precise, mild, and quietly complete.
```

## Negative constraint block

```text
Do not make Receipt run a full pitch journey or speak at length. Do not use too many receipt-roll panels that require artificial filler. Do not make him a literal human man, accountant stereotype, nervous clerk caricature, animal, Patch clone, Bit/Bot-style robot, or generic office worker. Do not make the audit trail decorative, optional, chaotic, or irrelevant. Do not use messy paperwork storms, unreadable tiny ledgers, real brand receipts, real tax forms, or broad comic action.
```

## QA gates

- Does Receipt already know the pitch?
- Is this an anti-recruitment rather than another long persuasion sequence?
- Are there three fat receipt rolls, not excessive panels?
- Does Receipt quietly head-shake and hand over the slip?
- Does the hero receipt show the canonical “You son of a gun / I’m in” format?
- Is Writ's variant treated as an observed equivalent rather than an error?
