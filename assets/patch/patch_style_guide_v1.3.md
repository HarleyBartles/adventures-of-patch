Patch Character Visual Design Guide v1.3

Patch is often shown navigating tasks and processes, following structured rules and cooperating with others to complete his work.

Version note: v1.3 hardens Patch's eye canon. Patch's eyes are identity-critical: simple black oval eyes with small white shine highlights only. Do not invent alternate eye styles.

Character Overview

Patch is stylised as a friendly autonomous software agent with a clean, modern look. He is drawn in an editorial vector style with thick, rounded outlines and soft shading. His body is compact and proportional, standing roughly mid-sized compared to an average human. Patch's head is round with a simple expressive face; two short antennae protrude from his hoodie, hinting at his digital nature.

Colour Palette

Keep Patch's hoodie and bag in the teal family, use the darker tones for pants, shadows, outlines and deeper backgrounds, and use the pale neutrals for clean workspace or presentation surfaces.

When a scene requires semantic feedback colours that are not in the contact sheet palette, use them sparingly as prop-level accents only. Approval, warning or rejection colours should not compete with Patch's teal identity, and they should not alter his hoodie, bag, pants, shoes, face, antennae or core silhouette.

Clothing and Accessories

Hoodie: Patch always wears a teal hoodie with a front pouch pocket and two antennae protruding from the hood. The hood is usually up, but may be down when Patch is at rest.

Crossbody bag: Patch carries a teal crossbody bag with a small greater-than symbol (`>`) on the flap. This bag holds his payloads, receipts, plans, records and rows. The bag strap always crosses his torso from shoulder to opposite hip; the bag is never worn over one shoulder like a tote or shoulder bag. The bag normally rests at Patch's right hip, but it may shift to his left hip when a composition needs to keep his body readable. In a continuous scene, immediate sequence, mirrored setup, or before/after comparison, the bag must not switch shoulder/hip sides unless the continuity of the scene clearly explains that movement. The bag should never appear both in front and behind Patch at the same time, including in mirror reflections. In reflected views, show the same single crossbody bag consistently, respecting the mirror orientation without duplicating it.

Pants and shoes: Dark pants and teal shoes with white soles anchor Patch's outfit. The shoes have rounded toes and simple detailing.

Eyes and Face

Patch's eyes are identity-critical.

Canonical Patch eyes:

- simple black oval eyes;
- small white shine highlights only;
- no white sclera or white eye fields;
- no visible iris or coloured pupil;
- no anime, human, animal, doll, toy, or generic mascot eye construction;
- no large expressive cartoon eyes that change the eye anatomy;
- no eyelid-heavy expressions that imply human eyes;
- no creative alternate eye styles.

The white mark in Patch's eye is a shine highlight, not a pupil and not a white eye area. Keep it small and consistent.

Patch's face is simple but expressive. Expression should come mainly from posture, head angle, mouth curve, body language, and simple brow/face marks when appropriate. Do not alter Patch's eye construction to create emotion.

Expression guidance:

- Curiosity: open posture, slight head tilt, attentive black oval eyes with small shine highlights.
- Determination: forward lean, focused body language, simple brow/face emphasis without changing eye anatomy.
- Uncertainty: small flat or uncertain mouth, cautious pose.
- Relief: gentle smile, relaxed pose.

Bag Placement and Continuity

Patch's bag has specific semantic rules because it is part of how audiences recognise him and read the scene:

- The bag is always a crossbody bag, never a one-shoulder bag.
- The strap must visibly cross Patch's torso from one shoulder to the opposite hip whenever his torso is visible.
- The bag usually rests at Patch's right hip.
- The bag may rest at Patch's left hip when composition requires it, especially to avoid blocking important body language or a foreground object.
- The bag must not switch sides within a sequence unless the scene clearly shows or implies that Patch has repositioned it.
- The bag must never appear as both a front bag and a back bag in the same frame.
- Mirror reflections must preserve the same single bag. The reflection may reverse orientation, but it must not create a second bag or imply that Patch is wearing two bags.
- When a frame is intended to be a comparison, a direct continuation, or the same moment shown in different contexts, keep the bag placement consistent between panels.

Expressions and Poses

Patch communicates through simple face marks, mouth curvature, posture and gesture while preserving canonical eye construction. Poses should feel natural and active: walking; pondering with a hand on his chin; checking items in his bag; or presenting documents or data to another person or system. Keep limbs proportionate and slightly rounded to match the overall aesthetic.

Scenes and Environments

Patch appears in various settings. Use these environment guidelines:

Interior spaces: Dim or warm interior settings, such as quiet workspaces or meeting rooms. Contrast Patch with lighter surfaces to keep him visible.

Exterior spaces: Urban or generic outdoor scenes with signage or access points. Light sources may reflect on Patch's hoodie and bag.

Preparation scenes: Bright indoor environments with neutral walls, off-white or light grey. Patch organises his bag or checks his reflection in a mirror before heading out.

Presentation scenes: Simple backgrounds that do not distract from Patch. Use subtle gradients or abstract shapes in light tones.

Do's and Don'ts

Do: Keep Patch's hoodie teal and his bag present in all action scenes.

Do: Maintain rounded, thick outlines and minimal shading.

Do: Use the defined colour palette when adding new props or accessories.

Do: Use black oval eyes with small white shine highlights only.

Don't: Depict Patch as aggressive or villainous.

Don't: Add complex patterns or logos that distract from the design.

Don't: Change the core proportions or facial features.

Don't: give Patch white sclera, visible irises, coloured pupils, anime eyes, human eyes, doll eyes, animal eyes, or alternate creative eye styles.

Prompt Guidance for Image Generation

When using an image generator to create new illustrations featuring Patch, follow these guidelines:

Describe Patch clearly: mention his teal hoodie, antennae, crossbody bag with a `>` symbol, dark pants, teal shoes with white soles, and simple black oval eyes with small white shine highlights only. Specify that the bag is worn crossbody, normally at his right hip, and that continuity must be preserved across panels or reflections.

Specify the style: request a clean, vector editorial style with thick outlines and soft shading.

Set the scene context: e.g. "preparing indoors," "entering a work area," "sharing documents or data with a colleague," or another deck-specific frame.

Avoid real brands or artists: keep prompts generic and original.

Example prompt:

Create a clean, vector-style illustration of a friendly autonomous software agent named Patch. Patch wears a teal hoodie with a front pocket and two small antennae on the hood. He has simple black oval eyes with small white shine highlights only, no white sclera and no irises. He carries a teal crossbody bag with a `>` symbol on the flap, worn crossbody from shoulder to opposite hip, and wears dark pants and teal shoes with white soles. Place Patch in a collaborative workspace, politely sharing documents or data with a larger colleague. Use a dark navy background with subtle lighting, thick rounded outlines and simple soft shading.
