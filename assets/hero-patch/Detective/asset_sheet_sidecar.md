Deterministically compiled with Pillow on the approved v4 template. Image generation was not used for the final sheet layout. Hero and alternate views use contain/no-crop placement.

## Guidance
- Keep Patch recognisable: teal hoodie, hood antennae, off-white face panel, black oval eyes and black crossbody bag with > mark.
- Detective role reads through the beige trench coat, detective cap, magnifying glass, note-taking and clue-hunting prop language.
- Do not replace the core Patch silhouette; the trench coat sits over the hoodie and the antennae remain visible through the cap design.
- Use side, back, action, detail and prop views as reference anchors for later scene generation and costume continuity.
- Preferred presentation is clean white-background source art with readable full-body, action, detail and prop views.
