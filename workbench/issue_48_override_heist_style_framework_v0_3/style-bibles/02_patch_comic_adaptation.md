## Source posture

This bible is repo-grounded for Patch identity and provisional for the comic-book heist adaptation layer. It preserves the combined v0.1 Patch section and carries forward the deck-shape idea that Patch begins in normal editorial vector style, shifts into comic-book heist thinking, then returns to editorial vector in the celebration.

## Asset overview

Patch remains the singular protagonist. The comic-book adaptation adds heist-world styling to Patch without changing his identity, anatomy, or core visual canon.

## Function and narrative role

Patch is the architect of the protected vault and the protagonist who needs lawful access. He must visibly reject brute force, assemble the crew, carry the programmed keycard, and complete the final scan. Patch should carry the lesson through action: lawful override succeeds when the route is designed, scoped, tested, recoverable, and auditable.

## Identity-critical canon

Patch must remain:

- friendly autonomous software-agent protagonist;
- compact and proportional;
- wearing a long-sleeved teal hoodie with hood up in most active scenes;
- teal hood framing an off-white face panel;
- simple black oval eyes with tiny white shine highlights only;
- two slim dark hood-integrated antennae with small rounded tips;
- dark trousers;
- teal shoes with white soles;
- teal crossbody bag with dark strap crossing shoulder-to-opposite-hip;
- dark/black bag flap or mark area with white `>` symbol;
- the only Patch in the scene.

## Comic-book adaptation rules

Allowed additions:

- slightly stronger ink emphasis;
- more dramatic action poses;
- heist-specific props held or carried by Patch;
- panel lighting and rim shadows;
- close-up hand/keycard shots that still preserve Patch when visible;
- small costume cues layered over canon only if they do not obscure hoodie, antennae, face panel, bag, trousers, shoes, or `>` mark.

Patch may appear more cinematic but not redesigned. The adaptation should read as “Patch in comic-book heist mode,” not “a superhero version of Patch.”

## Deck-stage treatment

Slide 1 should remain normal Patch editorial vector. Slide 2 can split normal editorial vector and comic-panel heist-thought language, including a zoom into Patch's eyes and moody low-angle thought panels. Recruitment and heist sections use comic-panel logic. Final celebration returns to canonical editorial vector so the comic-book style reads as Patch's playful mental model for serious lawful design work, not a permanent genre switch.

## Palette

Patch’s teal identity remains protected. Comic-book lighting may deepen shadows or add warm rim light, but should not recolour Patch’s hoodie, bag, trousers, shoes, face panel, antennae, eyes, or `>` symbol.

## Props and handling

Patch may hold the programmable override keycard, pitch binder, route plans, access papers, or small safehouse tools. Props must not cover identity-critical Patch features. If Patch’s torso is visible, the crossbody strap should be visible unless the composition makes it impossible and another strong Patch identity feature remains clear.

## Prompt-positive canon block

```text
Canonical Patch appears in comic-book heist mode while remaining the same friendly autonomous software-agent protagonist. Patch is compact and proportional, drawn in a clean Adventures editorial-vector style with thick rounded outlines, soft shading, and slightly stronger comic-book ink/shadow emphasis. Patch wears a long-sleeved teal hoodie with the hood up, a front pouch pocket, and two slim dark antenna stalks protruding from the hood, each ending in a small dark rounded tip. The teal hood frames an off-white face panel with simple black oval eyes and tiny white shine highlights only. Patch wears dark trousers and teal shoes with white soles. Patch carries a distinct teal crossbody bag with a dark strap crossing the torso from shoulder to opposite hip and a dark flap or mark area with a white `>` symbol. Patch may hold or scan a programmable override keycard, carry a pitch binder, review route plans, or move through comic-book heist panels, but must remain recognisably Patch.
```

## Prompt-negative canon block

```text
Do not redesign Patch into a superhero stranger, generic robot, screen-face bot, cute robot, toy mascot, plush character, human boy, anime/chibi character, glossy 3D character, photoreal character, or generic AI icon. Do not give Patch white sclera, irises, coloured pupils, visor eyes, glowing display eyes, large cartoon eyes, doll eyes, animal eyes, or alternate eye styles. Do not remove or hide the teal hoodie, hood antennae, off-white face panel, dark trousers, teal shoes with white soles, crossbody bag, visible strap, or white `>` bag mark. Do not create Patch clones or give other agents Patch’s hoodie, antennae, bag, face panel, teal identity palette, full silhouette, or `>` symbol. Do not show Patch bypassing, breaking, hacking, exploding, or defeating the vault.
```

## QA hard gates

- Does Patch read as canonical Patch, not a new heist character?
- Are the eyes simple black ovals with tiny white shine highlights only?
- Is the hoodie long-sleeved teal and the hood framing an off-white face panel?
- Are antennae present, slim, dark, hood-integrated, and modest?
- Are dark trousers and teal shoes with white soles present when visible?
- Is the crossbody bag distinct, with strap and `>` mark when torso/bag is visible?
- Is there only one Patch?
- Are all other agents clearly non-Patch?
- Does the comic-book treatment add staging without overwriting identity?
