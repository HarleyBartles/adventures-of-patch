## Asset overview

The Patch recruitment pitch folder is a cross-recruit continuity object. It begins as a practical folder and loose working list. As each specialist joins, Patch keeps a small physical assent marker from that recruit's domain. The finished folder is a record of recruitment and assent, not the legal proof or operational access object.

The folder and the keycard must remain distinct:

- **Pitch folder:** recruitment aid, memory object, and accumulated assent record.
- **Keycard:** later post-recruitment proof/access object that carries lawful override layers.

The folder may become visually busy by the final state, but it should still read as a handled recruitment folder rather than a proof ledger, audit dossier, evidence wall, threat list, or final access credential.

## Base folder state

The clean starting folder state is:

- warm manila / muted ochre folder with rounded corners and brass corner protectors;
- taped label reading `OVERRIDE HEIST / RECRUITMENT PITCH`;
- loose handwritten `Agents Needed` list placed on top of the folder;
- list entries:
  1. `Index`
  2. `Silk`
  3. `Rollback`
  4. `Writ`
  5. `Klause`
  6. `Receipt`
- list style: informal, handwritten, numbered, working-note energy;
- no checkboxes, columns, status table, or formal tracker structure.

The list is an external working note. It can be casually struck through as each recruit joins, but it should not become a spreadsheet, checklist form, or proof ledger.

## Progression state rule

Each folder-state reference should preserve all accepted previous markers and add only the next recruit's marker. The list strike-through state advances one name at a time.

- Clean state: no names struck through.
- Index state: Index struck through.
- Silk state: Index and Silk struck through.
- Rollback state: Index, Silk, and Rollback struck through.
- Writ state: Index, Silk, Rollback, and Writ struck through.
- Klause state: Index, Silk, Rollback, Writ, and Klause struck through.
- Receipt final state: all six names struck through.

Use casual handwritten strike-throughs only. Do not add checkbox columns or status columns to the base list.

## Marker locks

### Index marker — folded blueprint and sticky note

Index's recruitment path runs through files and archives. She eventually digs out a document that says she is in.

Folder-state behavior:

- A folded blue route/schematic blueprint is stored inside the closed folder.
- The blueprint is slightly oversized, so a visible blue folded edge peeks from the folder.
- The folder stays closed and intact.
- Index's name is casually struck through on the list.

Featured marker behavior:

- The full folded blueprint is shown as the right-panel marker detail.
- A sticky note on the blueprint reads: `you son of a gun. I'm in`.
- The blueprint should feel like recovered archive/source-route evidence, not a polished modern engineering poster.

### Silk marker — circular pin clipped to the blueprint

Silk's recruitment path runs through cracks, passageways, shafts, climbs, rappels, and route testing. The red thread is her through-line and resolves at a route endpoint.

Folder-state behavior:

- The circular Silk badge is clipped to the visible Index blueprint inside the folder.
- The badge is associated with the blueprint, not with the external `Agents Needed` list.
- The folder cover may naturally obscure part of the badge, but the blueprint should not pocket or cover the badge.
- A short red thread may remain visible when its placement makes that physically plausible.
- Silk's name is casually struck through on the list.

Featured marker behavior:

- Circular pin badge.
- Top rim text: `you son of a gun`.
- Bottom rim text: `I'm in`.
- Center icon: a simple steel rappel hoop with three red strings knotted to the hoop and pulled taut in three evenly spaced directions.
- Red thread/cord detail present.

Hard negatives:

- no spider/web read;
- no red-string conspiracy-board read;
- no badge clipped to the external list;
- no pocket/sleeve invented in the blueprint.

### Rollback marker — metal dog tags on chain

Rollback's recruitment domain is steel shutters, containment, test bunkers, and plan recovery. He is male.

Folder-state behavior:

- A metal chain emerges over or from the folder's top-left/spine area.
- Two scuffed metal dog tags hang outside the folder from that chain.
- The folder remains intact: no cutaway, slit, notch, pocket, lifted flap, or reshaped edge.
- The tags may be smaller and less readable on the folder-state side; full wording belongs in the featured marker view.
- Rollback's name is casually struck through on the list.

Featured marker behavior:

- Two brushed/scuffed metal dog tags.
- One tag reads `you son of a gun`.
- The other tag reads `I'm in`.
- They should read as industrial containment/test-bunker tags, not generic jewellery or military costume dominance.
- Supporting text should use male pronouns for Rollback, or avoid pronouns.

Hard negatives:

- no folder cutaway;
- no dog tags revealed through a hole in the folder;
- no military costume framing;
- no shiny decorative necklace read;
- no female-pronoun support text for Rollback.

### Writ marker — pink triplicate carbon-copy assent form

Writ's recruitment domain is fusty, compliant, and procedural. His folder contribution is the pink copy of a triplicate assent form. The pen, pad, white copy, yellow copy, and out tray are scene props only, not folder additions.

Folder-state behavior:

- The pink copy is filed beneath the folder cover, inside the folder.
- It protrudes below the intact bottom edge of the folder.
- The loose `Agents Needed` list does not hold, trap, cover, or occlude the pink slip.
- The visible pink strip shows `I'M IN` and Writ's signature.
- The folder edge is intact: no cutout, notch, slit, pocket, or flap.
- Writ's name is casually struck through on the list.

Featured marker behavior:

- The right panel may demonstrate the triplicate carbon-copy structure.
- White top copy: shows `OFFICIAL ASSENT` and the notes area with handwritten `you son of a gun`.
- White copy fold should hide its lower `I'M IN` and signature area.
- Pink copy beneath: shows only the bottom `I'M IN` and signature section as the received copy.
- Yellow copy beneath: only a subtle hint / strip with the signature-line area.
- The form layout should read as one triplicate stack, not different forms.

Hard negatives:

- no pen, pad, white copy, yellow copy, or out tray on the folder-state side;
- no pink slip under the external list;
- no full pink form displayed on the folder;
- no folder cutout to reveal the slip.

### Klause marker — direct produced stamp on the folder

Klause's assent is a decisive direct mark on the folder.

Folder-state behavior:

- The produced Klause stamp impression is on the folder cover, toward the bottom-left.
- When the list is present, the list partially obscures the stamp.
- The stamp remains a produced mark, not a separate stamp object.
- Klause's name is casually struck through on the list.

Featured marker behavior:

- Detail view of the same produced stamp impression.
- Top rim text: `you son of a gun`.
- Bottom rim text: `I'm in`.
- Center icon: a stylised, simple, efficiently executed letter `K`.

Hard negatives:

- no rubber stamp tool;
- no hand;
- no ink pad;
- no giant generic `APPROVED` gag;
- no wax-seal fantasy.

### Receipt marker — thermal receipt-roll summary

Receipt closes the recruitment sequence by producing a narrow thermal-paper receipt from a receipt roll.

Folder-state behavior:

- A narrow thermal-paper receipt is stapled near the top-right of the folder cover.
- It is slightly curled but fully legible.
- It sits over the list and partially obscures the right side of the list.
- All six names on the `Agents Needed` list are struck through in the final folder state.

Featured marker behavior:

- Detail view of the receipt alone.
- The receipt lists the previous five agents as `IN`:
  - Index — IN
  - Silk — IN
  - Rollback — IN
  - Writ — IN
  - Klause — IN
- Receipt's own line appears at the bottom: `you son of a gun. I'm in`.
- The paper should read as thermal receipt roll: narrow, slightly curled, printed, and practical.

Hard negatives:

- no full audit ledger;
- no legal proof document;
- no keycard/access authorization;
- no spreadsheet/table form;
- no receipt acting as operational proof rather than recruitment assent summary.

## Overall material language

The finished folder should show six distinct material languages:

- Index: folded blueprint / archive route document.
- Silk: circular route-end pin with red thread and steel hoop icon.
- Rollback: scuffed metal chain and tags.
- Writ: pink carbon-copy form slip.
- Klause: direct ink stamp on folder cover.
- Receipt: stapled thermal-paper receipt roll.

This variety is important. Each marker should feel like it came from that recruit's own domain rather than from a generic matching stationery set.

## QA hard gates

- Does the folder/keycard boundary remain clear?
- Does the object read as recruitment assent memory, not proof/access?
- Does the base list stay a loose handwritten numbered note rather than a formal tracker?
- Are strike-through states correct for the recruitment stage?
- Is each marker tied to the correct recruit domain and material language?
- Is the folder intact, with no cutaways, notches, slits, pockets, or invented reveal geometry?
- Is Silk's badge clipped to the blueprint rather than the external list?
- Is Rollback's marker industrial/scuffed metal rather than military costume or jewellery?
- Is Writ's pink slip under the folder cover and not under the list?
- Is Klause represented by the produced stamp only, not a stamping object?
- Is Receipt's thermal-paper receipt a recruitment summary only?
- Is the final folder accumulated but still readable?

## Repair guidance

If the list becomes formal: remove checkboxes, status columns, grids, or form language and restore a loose handwritten numbered `Agents Needed` note.

If a marker causes folder cutaways: keep the folder silhouette intact and show the marker protruding, clipped, hanging, stamped, stapled, or tucked using physically plausible external layering.

If Writ's pink slip appears under the list: move it clear of the list and make the folder cover the occluding layer; it should protrude below the intact folder edge.

If Rollback tags force a cutaway: hang the tags externally from chain over the top-left/spine area instead of revealing them through the folder body.

If Silk's badge loses the route-end logic: restore the central steel rappel hoop with three red strings pulled taut in thirds.

If Receipt becomes too formal: make it narrow thermal paper with practical printed lines and a slight curl, not a ledger or authorization sheet.
